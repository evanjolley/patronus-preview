Behavioral alignment is the next frontier of AI

Agents learn judgment on the job

They have enough knowledge to do the work. What they lack is judgment, which humans learn on the job and don’t always write down.

We build worlds for agents to practice

Each one is modeled on a real working environment, where information is fragmented, user requests conflict, and not every requirement is stated.

We study what agents do

When an agent misses the point or cuts corners, we figure out why. Then we build the training data to correct it.

Research comes first

We are fundamental researchers and domain experts. Join us
