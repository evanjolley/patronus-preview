Behavioral alignment is the next frontier of AI

Models learned the world by reading about it

Nearly everything they know came from text, and this took them remarkably far.

Behavior has to be learned by doing

Real work arrives late, spreads across tools and contradicts itself. You cannot read your way to quality judgment in ambiguous circumstances.

So we build worlds for models to explore

Each one is grounded in real work, then scaled into an environment as detailed and expansive as the job itself.

And we study what they do there

When a model cuts a corner or loses the thread, we find out why and build the data that brings its behavior in line.

Every domain asks for different judgment

We start where models are expected to do real work and go deepest where they fail.

AlignmentAlignment→Follow instructions and know when to push back.CodingCoding→Ship a feature in code no one documented.Knowledge WorkKnowledge Work→Make one decision from sources that disagree.ScienceScience→Identify and explore the untested question.Computer UseComputer Use→Get work done in software built for people.
