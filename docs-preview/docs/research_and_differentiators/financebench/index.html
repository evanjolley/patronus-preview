About

Open-book financial question answering

FinanceBench is a test suite for evaluating LLMs on open-book financial question answering. It comprises 10,231 questions about publicly traded companies, with corresponding answers and evidence strings. In the paper, GPT-4-Turbo used with a retrieval system incorrectly answered or refused to answer 81% of a sample of 150 cases.
