## Questions about buying environments

### Where can I buy RL environments, off the shelf or built to order?

Patronus AI sells both. It has a set of ready-made RL environments, but most of our work is custom. We build the tasks and problems in each environment, and we can custom-build environments calibrated to your specific needs and requirements.

### Can I buy app simulations or mock websites for agent training?

Yes, you can buy parts of an RL environment on their own. An RL environment is made of parts, such as simulated apps, mock websites and the data behind them. Tell us which parts you need, and we will scope the rest.

### Who owns the training data from a custom build?

Ownership of the training data from a custom build is set in each agreement. We take on your hardest problems, and the data we deliver is built for your needs.

### Can your RL environments run on our own training harness?

It’s possible. Tell us about your training harness when we scope the work, and we’ll confirm how tasks can run in your setup.

### How long does a custom RL environment take from scoping to delivery?

It varies with scope. We usually send samples first and go from there, and our researchers work closely with your research team so the data meets our quality bar.

### How do I judge the quality of an RL environment?

Look for expert review and automated checks. Every Patronus AI environment goes through rigorous manual reviews by domain experts and automated checks before delivery.

### How do I buy RL environments from Patronus AI?

Start with a note through our [contact page](https://www.patronus.ai/contact) with the domain and what you need, and our team will follow up to scope the work.

### Should I build or buy RL environments?

It depends on what your team can staff. An RL environment needs tasks, simulated apps or mock websites, the data behind them and a reward, and each part needs people who know the domain. Buying from Patronus AI gets you environments reviewed by domain experts and checked before delivery, ready-made or custom-built, and you can buy only the parts you need.

### What does an RL environment include?

An RL environment is a sandbox with the tools an agent needs, a task and a reward that scores the result. It is made of parts, such as a codebase or simulated apps, mock websites and the data behind them. For computer use, expert trajectories can show how a skilled person moves through the task.
