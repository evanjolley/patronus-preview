# Research and engineering under one roof

> Patronus AI is a behavioral research lab for AI founded in 2023 by Anand Kannappan and Rebecca Qian. We study where models fail and how to improve them.

Source: https://www.patronus.ai/company  
Publisher: Patronus AI, a behavioral research lab for AI (https://www.patronus.ai/)

Patronus AI was founded in 2023 by Anand Kannappan and Rebecca Qian. Our researchers and engineers come from Meta Superintelligence Labs, Amazon AGI, Apple and Google.

## Mission

Patronus AI is a behavioral research lab for AI. We build [worlds for models to explore](https://www.patronus.ai/domains) and study what they do there and why. Our work combines [open-ended research](https://www.patronus.ai/research) with hands-on engineering to meet difficult problems on the frontier of AI.
