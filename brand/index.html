02 · Clear space and size

Room to breathe

A quarter of the cube, all round

Keep a margin of one quarter of the cube’s height on every side of the mark or lockup. Keep text, edges and images out of it. Every PNG in the kit already carries it.

¼ cube
  • 16 pxThe cube alone, smallest. Use the favicon drawing, hinted to the 16 px grid.
  • 16–27 pxThe hairline cube. 24 px keeps its line sharp.
  • 28 px +The display cube, and always the display cube inside a lockup.
  • 78 px wideThe horizontal “Patronus” lockup, smallest (96 px for “Patronus AI”).

03 · Misuse

Don’ts

Draw it the one way

  • Don’t rotate or tilt it
  • Don’t mirror it
  • Don’t recolor it
  • Don’t stretch it
  • No fills, shadows or glows
  • Never on a photograph
