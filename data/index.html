What we look for

We want the working history of a company, from several systems, for the same years, with the versions and threads still attached. A final report on its own is not enough. The drafts, approvals and conversations around it are what we look for.

Company records

The accounting system with its ledgers, reconciliations and close packages. Spreadsheets and documents as they changed, such as forecasts, models and memos. Approvals and the email or chat that led to them. Trackers and tickets, CRM history, and more.

Private codebases

A production product a business ran on for years, with its full git history, reviewed pull requests, tests and the tracker that goes with it, and more. Design documents and specs that explain the code are welcome too. Systems in languages with little public code, such as COBOL, Fortran, Erlang or Rust, are a priority.

AI agent traces

If your company runs AI agents in production, the full record of each run with its reasoning, tool calls and outcome, along with the agent’s codebase.

Who has this

Software companies, including ones that have wound down and kept their archives. Distributors, manufacturers, labs, logistics firms, banks, insurers, regional firms and more, with ten years or more of history. Smaller businesses too, when their records span several years and at least two systems.

What we don’t license

Material your company does not own, such as client books or client code. Code that was ever public or was mostly generated by AI tools. Clinical healthcare data. Screen recordings and video.

Why Patronus

Patronus AI is a behavioral research lab for AI that studies where models fail and builds training data and simulated environments to improve them. Models learn language from public text, but the judgment people use on the job is rarely written down. Company records hold that judgment, in the decisions, revisions and threads behind each result.

We are the buyer, not a broker. You sign with Patronus and are paid by Patronus. We reply within two business days with a fit decision or our next questions, and we give you a written record of everything we receive, remove and delete.

Rights, consent and privacy

  • You confirm you can authorize it

    We work with the person who can sign for the material, usually the owner, CEO or CFO. You tell us about any client, counterparty or privilege restrictions before anything is shared. If an adviser or partner introduces you, you still confirm with us directly.

  • Your sample is used only for evaluation

    We don’t train on it, resell it or use it for anything else. Named reviewers work with it in an isolated environment.

  • Personal information is removed before any use

    Names of customers and staff, and other personal information, are removed before the material is used or delivered, and you get a report of what was removed. Secrets such as keys and passwords are removed from code.

  • Your company is never named

    Licensed material becomes training and evaluation material without your company’s name, and nobody outside Patronus sees the raw records.

  • Deletion with proof

    If we don’t proceed, we delete the originals and working copies and send written confirmation with a fingerprint of what was deleted. If we do proceed, originals are deleted on the schedule agreed in the license.

  • Non-exclusive by default

    You keep the material and can keep using it. Exclusivity is available but never required.

  • Security and compliance

    Patronus AI is SOC 2, HIPAA and TISAX compliant.

Questions about licensing data

Does Patronus AI buy my company’s data or license it?

Patronus AI licenses it. Your company keeps its records and code and can keep using them. A license gives Patronus permission to use a defined scope of material for agreed purposes. Licenses are non-exclusive unless you choose otherwise.

Who needs to consent before a company licenses its data to Patronus AI?

The person who can authorize a license for all of the material, usually the owner, CEO or CFO, signs with Patronus AI. Before any sample is shared, they confirm they are authorized and list any client, counterparty or privilege restrictions. Material a company does not own, such as client files or client code, is not eligible.

How does Patronus AI de-identify licensed data?

Personal information, such as customer and staff names, is removed before Patronus AI uses or delivers licensed material, and the company gets a report of what was removed. Secrets such as keys and passwords are removed from code. Reviewers also check that records still connect to each other after de-identification.

Who sees my data while Patronus AI evaluates it?

Only named Patronus AI reviewers, in an isolated environment. A sample is shared only after both sides agree to evaluation terms, and it is used for evaluation alone. Patronus does not train on a sample or resell it.

Who owns the data after I license it to Patronus AI?

Your company still owns its records and code. A license is not a sale, so you keep the material and keep using it. The license sets the scope Patronus AI may use and for what purposes, such as AI training, evaluation, research and building tasks and environments.

Do I have to license my data to Patronus AI exclusively?

No. Patronus AI licenses data non-exclusively by default, so you can license the same material to others. Exclusivity is available if you want it and is priced separately. Patronus asks every owner whether the material has been licensed before, and material already licensed exclusively to someone else is not eligible.

What happens to my data after I license it to Patronus AI?

Patronus AI reviews the full dataset against the acceptance criteria in the license, and personal information is removed. The material then becomes training and evaluation material, without your company’s name. Nobody outside Patronus sees the raw records. Originals and working copies are deleted on the schedule agreed in the license, with confirmation.

What happens if Patronus AI decides not to license my data?

Patronus AI deletes the sample’s originals and working copies and sends you written confirmation with a fingerprint of what was deleted. If it is not a fit at the first step, Patronus says so plainly, and no files have been shared.

How does payment work when I license data to Patronus AI?

Patronus AI pays the company directly, on acceptance of the full dataset. The price, payment terms and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing after a sample review. An initial range is only an estimate. Patronus prices at the level of the published market, based on things like company size, years of history and the systems the records come from. If a data partner introduced you, Patronus pays the partner from its own side, never out of your share.

What kinds of data does Patronus AI license?

Patronus AI licenses two kinds of material. One is company records, meaning accounting systems, spreadsheets and documents with their versions, approvals, email and chat, trackers, and more, from several systems for the same years. The other is private codebases with full history, reviewed pull requests, tests and the tracker that goes with them, and more. Patronus also looks for traces from AI agents running in production. It does not license public code, code mostly generated by AI, client-owned material, clinical healthcare data, or screen recordings and video.
