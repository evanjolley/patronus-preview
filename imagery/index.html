The Grand Canyon set

One stretch of the canyon below Bright Angel, recorded four ways and registered to the same ground. Map and orthoimagery aligned from their coordinates; photo aligned to them by feature matching and a smooth local warp (the photo is oblique, so expect small residual offsets).
